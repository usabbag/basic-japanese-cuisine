Invented in the 1980s by sushi chefs in California for customers who didn't want visible seaweed.

  1. Bigger mat; alcohol, then cling film over it.
  2. Avocado into 16 pieces. Peel the cucumber with the yanagi. Video: cucumber salt-rubbed, 8 cm lengths, katsura-muki then julienne; avocado rolled around the stone and peeled.video 9-4
  3. ~100–120 g rice spread fluffy over the whole nori, same spreading technique but top to bottom; sesame; flip rice-side down.video 9-4
  4. Fillings in the middle, crab stick at the top / avocado centred first, then crab, bean sprouts, cucumber last.video 9-4
  5. Wrap the fillings with the front edge first, then roll down. Cling film over, shape square with the mat.video 9-4
  6. 6 pieces: halve, align, trim ends, slightly slanted cuts.video 9-4
