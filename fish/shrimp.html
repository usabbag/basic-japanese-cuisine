Akaebi — Argentine red shrimp, raw

  1. Twist the head once to separate it from the body.video 9-7
  2. Peel, leaving the tail joint in place; go all the way around.video 9-7
  3. Sebiraki (butterfly from the back): open from the back without splitting all the way through.video 9-7
  4. Remove the sewata (vein) thoroughly, as with boiled shrimp.video 9-7
  5. Wash the vein area clean in 2% salt water until no trace of the vein remains.video 9-7
  6. Wipe off the moisture thoroughly. Into the counter case in this state.video 9-7

Shrimp / squid paste mushroom tempura

  1. 800 g of fish, squid or ebi, with egg white, made into a paste.notes
  2. Remove the mushroom stem, slit the cap lightly, dust the inside with all-purpose flour, fill with the paste.notes
