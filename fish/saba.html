Saba
鯖 saba · mackerel · maquereau

Filleting

  1. Remove the fin on the belly side.
  2. Head left. Slice tail to head, left fingers on top, knife at 45°.
  3. On the skin side, remove 1 cm of the darker part. Same for the other slice.
