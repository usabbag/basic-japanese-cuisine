Kinmedai
金目鯛 kinmedai · alfonsino · béryx

Scaling & gutting · Filleting · Yubiki · Sashimi · Bones & broth · For tempura

Scaling & gutting

  1. Scale inside a big plastic bag, opened only halfway — the scales are small and fly everywhere. Use a scaler.
  2. Scale again under running water.
  3. Scale a third time with the deba, blade inclined so it doesn't damage the flesh.
  4. Insert the knife diagonally next to the pectoral fin, angled towards the head, and slide towards the right side of the belly fin (uwami side).
  5. Cut the spine when the head is cut; pull the head to bring the guts out with it.
  6. Open the belly delicately. Remove the bloodline under running water with the back of the tweezers.
  7. Remaining guts: cut the stomach and pull — easier that way.
  8. Split the head: chin facing away from you. Knife inside the mouth, hold the head firmly with a cloth, slide down.

Filleting 3-piece

  1. Shitami as usual.
  2. Raise the thin belly meat: incision above the fin, head to tail, staying above the bones.
  3. Rotate. Tail to head: cut the skin lightly first, then slow pulling cuts. Open to check.
  4. Insert the knife at the tail; tail to head, slide along the cut and detach from the bones.
  5. Uwami: belly up, head to tail, on the bone.
  6. Rotate. Belly down, from the tail, above the fin.
  7. Gambara (belly bones) next.
  8. Discard the tail piece — like the fin areas, it can carry bacteria.

Yubiki / yushimo boiling-water drip

  • Eaten skin-on: the skin is tasty.
  • Shitami side for sashimi. Cover the skin with a paper towel, pour boiling water over it from a ladle, then straight into ice.

Sashimi

  • The tail part is chewy — it goes in the soup instead.
  • Start slicing from the tail; use the knife to move each slice to the right.

Bones & broth

  1. Salt the bones about 15 min, then rinse the salt off.
  2. Head: blanch 5 s, into iced water, then remove the remaining scales well under running water. Then simmer: Nizakana.
  3. Broth: start from cold water with kombu, bones in from cold. Remove the kombu when it boils.
  • Rice dish with the fillets: Flavoured rice with kinmedai.

Filleting — for tempura

  • Noted as "sardine-like fish for tempura": same opening method as a small fish.
  1. Cut off the head; remove the guts by pulling them out with the knife.
  2. Head to tail, slide the knife over the spine and cut through the rib (gambara) bones.
  3. Turn over and slide over the spine on the other side.
  4. Leave the skin attached.
  5. Pull the spine out, cut it off, and remove the belly bones.
  • Batter, frying and tendon: see Tempura. Dipping sauce: Tentsuyu.
