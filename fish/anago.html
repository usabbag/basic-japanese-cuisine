Anago
穴子 anago · conger eel · congre

Prep

  1. Pierce the eye to pin it. Knife at 45° upward, small moves, above the fin.
  2. About 1/3 from the head, index finger on the tip, pull. Don't pierce the skin.
  3. Knife upside down to open a gap between flesh and bone; follow with the left index.
  4. “Absolutely horrible fish to prep.”

Ni-anago — simmering liquid

Serving

  • Nigiri: flame the anago on the flesh side.
  • Anago donburi: shari, flamed anago, chopped cucumber, chopped gari, kanpyo, nori strips.
  • Anakyu roll (anago + cucumber) — method not recorded.
