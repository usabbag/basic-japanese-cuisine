Cuttlefish
甲烏賊 kō-ika · cuttlefish (not squid) · seiche

Sources: class notes 22 Oct. No course video. Draft.

Prep

  1. Tentacles up. Push down to pop the shell (cuttlebone) out.notes
  2. Open — notes say “Open by?”.notes

Slit the body open lengthwise where the shell was and lay it flat so the inside is exposed.

  1. Remove the ink sac. “Squid ink” in shops is actually cuttlefish ink.notes
  2. Remove the guts by pulling the thin membrane.notes
  3. Skin side up. Insert a finger under the skin layer and peel it off.notes

Serving

  • Ika to himo no aemono (cuttlefish-and-tentacle dressed salad): blanched tentacles, leek, cucumber, sesame oil, a bit of spice.notes
