Nigiri 握り

Targets (exam)

ShapeWeightTime
Slice (kiritsuke)3 × 7 cm10–12 g15 slices → best 10, in 3 min
Nigirisee chart22–24 g10 pieces in 2 min

Scoring: slicing ×2 and nigiri ×3, each /20 = shape 10 + weight 10. Minus points for bad cutting.

Shape chart

  • Good: right and left symmetric; beautiful curve line; fan shape from the front; sides solid; slightly slender from above; shari not overflowing the neta.
  • Bad: neta centre misaligned; skewed; straight line (no curve); too roundish; height too low; loose on the sides; uneven; too long; push from top and bottom not enough; shari overflowing.
  • “Koba” (the cut face) is face-up when you prepare the nigiri.

Forming (right-handed)

  1. Rice in the edobitsu (cypress tub, wetted). It's a little above 35 °C; by serving it's ~35 °C.video 3-5
  2. Tezu on three fingertips (rice page). Rice doesn't touch the palm.video 3-5
  3. Right hand takes the rice; left hand takes the fish. Rice onto the back of the fish.video 3-5
  4. Left thumb presses a shallow dimple in the centre of the rice; right index and thumb hold the tip and base so the rice doesn't stretch.video 3-5
  5. Release the thumb, lay the right index on the rice; close the left palm a little so the fish sticks to the rice.video 3-5
  6. Open and flip with index and middle finger, pushing rice and fish together toward the fingertips: fish now on top.video 3-5
  7. Close the two “legs” of rice with middle finger and thumb; back into the pocket between palm and fingers.video 3-5
  8. Fold the fingers to hold the sides, thumb under the fish aligning the far end, two right fingers cover the top to shape.video 3-5
  9. Rotate clockwise with middle finger and thumb; same closure. Once more for the other side. Done.video 3-5
  • Edo style: incision on the right-hand side.
  • More wasabi on fatty tuna.
  • Many decorations possible on kohada; head top-left, slice shallowly.

Hosomaki 細巻き · thin roll

Norihalf sheet
Rice80 g (75 g on one day)
Cucumber≈ 16–17 g per stick, 0.5 cm shorter than the nori each side; cut in half; keep the triangle while shaving
Kanpyo3–4 strings ≈ 10–11 g
Exam2 cucumber rolls + 1 kanpyo roll in 5 min (6 max)
Cutcucumber: square section, 6 pieces. Kanpyo: tunnel shape, 4 pieces
  1. Cucumber: start the cut at the tip, then one motion. Sticks 1 cm below the nori edge.
  2. Make an oval with the rice, spread down with the left hand left to right, then spread down.
  3. Rice to 90% up, one finger of bare nori at the top; a little extra rice just below that gap. Rice ~1 cm thick with a “little mountain” ridge; 3 mm at the edges.
  4. First roll the mat bottom to top, leaving the gap, then another roll. Cylinder.
  5. 1 roll → 6: cut in half, then thirds.

Gunkan 軍艦

  • 15–18 g rice, low and wide.
  • Keep the nori in the bamboo mat so it stays dry. Stick it to 2/3, leave 1/3 empty; stretch the nori if needed.
  • Wasabi on top or inside (inside preferred).

Temaki 手巻き · hand roll

  • 30 g rice.
  • Left hand dry; hold the nori with the fingertips. Place rice diagonally, top-left to bottom-right.
  • Fold bottom-left to the top edge. A grain of rice closes it.

Box sushi 押し寿司 · battera, with kohada

  1. Cover both non-frame parts with plastic. Wet the frame.
  2. Squeeze the moisture from gari, roll it in shiso, chop finely.
  3. Fish skin-side down; the other piece blood-lines out. Use the small bits to complete the layer.
  4. 200 g rice per box: lightly compact 120 g, make a hollow along the centre line, press the four corners.
  5. A light layer of the shiso/gari mix + sesame.
  6. Then 80 g rice; press from the corners in. Push, rotate 45°, push again.
  7. Out of the frame; lay the kombu on top. Cut in the middle, then 3 pieces each.

Osaka-style: sandwich between paper and film; keeps in the fridge up to 2 nights. → Kohada
