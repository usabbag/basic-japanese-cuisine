Anisakis

  • Parasite of open-ocean fish: saba, surume-ika, buri (bigger fish, bigger worms). Enters via whale droppings → small fish → big fish.video 1-1
  • Lives in the guts while the fish is fresh; migrates through the bloodline into the flesh as freshness drops. Poor-freshness fish have it in the meat.video 1-1
  • Visible to the naked eye: look and remove.video 1-1
  • Killed by −20 °C for 24 h, or 60 °C for 1 min.video 1-1
  • Attached to the stomach wall it causes severe pain; only a hospital can remove it.video 1-1

Equipment

  • Secondary contamination: a germ on the knife ends up on the food. Knives, boards, tools washed and sanitised every time.video 1-2
  • Cutting boards: chlorine bleach. Neta case: washed daily, alcohol spray.video 1-2
  • Fridge: clean and organised to the back. Never mix ingredients bought on different days.video 1-2
  • Cloths in at least three colours: fish prep · post-prep (slicing, nigiri) · general cleaning. The fish cloth smells; keep it apart.video 1-2
  • Pest control: professional service monthly.video 1-2
  • Vomit in the restroom: sanitise the room and the cleaning tools; the restroom cloth washed, sanitised, stored apart.video 1-2

The cook

  • Nails short; wash to the elbows; alcohol spray after washing.video 1-3
  • White uniform, no wrinkles; rubber apron for prep, fresh white apron for service; hat hides the fringe.video 1-3
  • Sleep and health are self-management; a tired cook seasons saltier.video 1-3
  • Taste before serving; check for bones.video 1-3
  • Check fingers and nails constantly.video 1-3

Antibacterial ingredients

  • Vinegar and soy: kill Vibrio and E. coli; Edo-period preservation before refrigeration (zuke).video 1-4
  • Wasabi: allyl isothiocyanate; stops germination. Storing food with wasabi in the fridge slows bacteria.video 1-4
  • Gari: strong antibacterial action, boosted by the sweet-vinegar marinade. → Garivideo 1-4
  • Green tea: catechin; cleans the palate of fish fat and smell.video 1-4
  • Bamboo leaf: salicylic acid; wrapping and decoration.video 1-4
