Kanpyo
かんぴょう · simmered gourd strips

Dried kanpyo g

250 gdried kanpyo
540 mldark soy
50 mlsake
600 gsugar
50 mlmirin
  1. Rub with salt, wash.
  2. Boil 20–30 min. Drain.
  3. Cook in the liquid 15–20 min.

Hosomaki: 3–4 strings ≈ 10 g per roll.
