Gari
ガリ · pickled ginger

New ginger g

350 gnew (young) ginger
200 ccwater
100 ccvinegar
50 gsugar
10 gsalt
  1. Young ginger; old ginger is spicier and harder. Peel with a spoon; trim the pink offshoots and dry ends.
  2. Slice parallel to the fibres, 1 mm.
  3. 3% salt water, 10 min.
  4. Drain; boil in plain water 5 min (counted from the return to the boil).
  5. Marinate in the vinegar liquid.
  • Keeps 1 month in water, 1 year in vinegar.
  • Same method works with green papaya / palm heart.
