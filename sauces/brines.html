塩水 shio-mizu · salt water and vinegar-water for fish prep

Sources: class notes, gari handout, video 9-7.

Ratio

Water ml

20 gsalt — 2%: washing shrimp and scallop meatvideo 9-7 · 9-6
30 gsalt — 3%: holding prepped fish, cleaning fillets, gari slices (10 min)notes · handout
35 gsalt — 3.5% “like the sea”: rinsing aji fillets 1 min (300 g water / 9 g salt in class)notes
300 mlvinegar in 700 ml water — 30/70 vinegar-water: aji surface, 1 minnotes

Used for

  • Kohada: fillets held in 3% salt water after cutting.notes → Kohada
  • Aji: 3.5% salt water 1 min, then 30/70 vinegar-water 1 min, before de-skinning.notes → Aji
  • Gari: 3% salt water 10 min before blanching.handout → Gari
  • Shrimp and scallop: washed in 2% salt water, never plain water.video 9-7 · 9-6 → Shrimp · Scallop

Salt water instead of fresh water keeps the flesh from absorbing water and going soft; it also lifts slime and residual blood. Use cold water, and never leave fish in it longer than stated.
