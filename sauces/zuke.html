Zuke
漬け · soy marinade

Sources: Class notes 13 Oct; video 8-1. Draft.

Ratio

Soy

2 soy
1 mirin
1 sake

Method

  1. Evaporate the alcohol.notes
  2. Blanch the tuna 5 seconds until the surface whitens, then ice water. Stops the soy going too deep and too salty.notes
  3. Wipe dry. Into a bowl, a ladle of zuke, paper towel on top. 1–2 h.notes

Used for

  • Tuna akami — originally a preservation method before refrigeration, now a cooking method.video 8-1 → Tuna
  • Zuke nigiri, zuke-don.curriculum

Works on any lean red fish (bonito, katsuo); not for fatty cuts, which repel the marinade.
