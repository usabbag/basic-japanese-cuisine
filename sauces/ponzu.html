Ponzu
ポン酢

Sources: Class notes 26 Oct. Draft.

Ratio

Citrus juice ml

500 mlcitrus juice — yuzu, daidai or sudachi
500 mldark soy
42 mlmirin
10 gkombu
20 gkatsuobushi

Method

  1. Steep together in the fridge, 1 week minimum.notes
  2. Keeps for months in the fridge; ideally vacuum-packed.notes

Used for

  • White fish (hirame usuzukuri, kombu-jime).notes → Hirame
  • Cooked dishes.notes

Fugu usuzukuri, shabu-shabu, tataki, grilled mushrooms. Add grated daikon and chives (momiji-oroshi for heat).
