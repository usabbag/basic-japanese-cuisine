Karaage marinade
唐揚げ · soy-sake-mirin marinade for frying

Ratio · Method · Used for
  • Basic marinade. Written up in class for fugu karaage (ふぐ唐); the same marinade works for chicken.

Ratio

Dark soy

2 dark soy (Yamasa)
2 sake
1 mirin
+grated ginger, a little

Method

  1. Marinate the pieces in the mixture. Time not recorded.
  2. Coat and deep-fry. Coating and oil temperature not recorded.

Typical practice: 15–30 min in the marinade, drain, dust in katakuriko (potato starch), fry at 170–180 °C; chicken thigh gets a second fry at 190 °C to crisp.

Used for

  • Fugu karaage (ふぐ唐揚げ).
  • Chicken karaage.
