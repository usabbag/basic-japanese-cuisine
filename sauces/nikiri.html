Nikiri soy
煮切り醤油 nikiri-shōyu

Ratio

Dark soy

7 dark soy
0.5 sake
0.5 mirin
+bonito flakes

Used for

  • Brushed on nigiri before serving, in place of the customer dipping.

Brushed on anago and tamago nigiri; a few drops on sashimi plates. Alcohol is burnt off (nikiru = to boil down) so it reads less sharp than raw soy.
