Plum sauce
煎酒 irizake-style

Sources: Handwritten note 28 Oct, next to a bottle of irizake. No method recorded. Draft.

Ratio

Sake

10 sake
2 plum (umeboshi)
1 kombu

Used for

  • Nagaimo for vegetable nigiri (handout lists bainiku, plum paste).handout → Vegetable nigiri

Irizake is the Edo-period seasoning that predates soy: simmer sake with umeboshi and kombu, reduce, strain. Used on white fish sashimi and tamago.
