Happō-ji
八方地 · dashi marinade

Sources: Handout 28 Oct. Draft.

Ratio

Dashi

7 dashi
1 light soy
1 mirin

Used for

  • Okra and shiitake for vegetable nigiri → Vegetable nigiri.handout

“Happō” = all directions: the general-purpose simmering liquid for vegetables (ohitashi, nibitashi). Blanch, then steep cold.
