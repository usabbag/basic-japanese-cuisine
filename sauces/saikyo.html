Saikyo-zuke
西京漬け · white miso cure

Sources: Board 19 Oct; class notes. Draft.

Ratio

Saikyo miso g

2000 gsaikyo (white) miso
400 mlsake
400 mlmirin
200 gsugar

Method

  1. Fish pieces 80–100 g (kaiseki: 60 g).notes
  2. Salt 10 min, rinse.notes
  3. Marinate 3 days.notes
  4. Grill (saikyo-yaki). No grilling notes taken.curriculum

Used for

  • Salmon saikyo-yaki, 19 Oct.curriculum → Salmon

Gindara (black cod), sawara, mero. Scrape the miso off before grilling or it burns; grill skin side first on low heat.
