Yuan-yaki
幽庵焼き · yuzu-soy marinade for grilling

Ratio · Method · Used for

Ratio

Dark soy

1 dark soy
1 sake
1 mirin
+yuzu skin and juice

Method

  1. Marinate 30 min.
  2. Grill. Grilling method not recorded.

Used for

  • Salmon yuan-yaki. → Salmon

Classic for buri, sawara, tai, chicken thigh. Yuzu can be replaced by sudachi or lemon zest.
