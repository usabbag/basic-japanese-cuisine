Yuan-yaki
幽庵焼き · yuzu-soy marinade for grilling

Sources: Board 19 Oct. Draft.

Ratio

Dark soy

1 dark soy
1 sake
1 mirin
+yuzu skin and juice

Method

  1. Marinate 30 min.board
  2. Grill. No grilling notes taken.curriculum

Used for

  • Salmon yuan-yaki, 19 Oct.curriculum → Salmon

Classic for buri, sawara, tai, chicken thigh. Yuzu can be replaced by sudachi or lemon zest.
