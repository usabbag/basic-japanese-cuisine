Tempura & tendon
天ぷら · 天丼

Ratio

Water cc

1000 ccwater
1 egg yolk

= egg-water. Then per 360 cc egg-water, ~200 g wheat flour.

Method

  1. Very light dusting of flour on the ingredients.
  2. Mix egg-water and flour very lightly. Do not over-mix, do not smooth.
  3. Ready when the frying sound drops in tone and the bubbles get smaller.

Fish/shrimp paste mushrooms

  • 800 g fish / squid / ebi, with egg white.
  • Remove the mushroom stem, slit the cap lightly, dust the inside with flour, fill with paste.

Serving

  • Dipping: tentsuyu.
  • Tendon: tempura over rice with don-tsuyu.

Keep the batter cold and mix it just before use; 170–180 °C for seafood, lower for vegetables.
