Soy-simmered hamachi
鰤のあら煮 · buri no ara-ni · collars and trimmings in dashi-soy

Source: handwritten note and photos, 6 Nov 2026. Different from the class nizakana: dashi instead of water + sake, far more soy, a long low simmer instead of a 15-min boil-down.

  • Uses the ara — collars (kama), belly trim, pieces on the bone, skin on — left after filleting a hamachi. Cut into large chunks, 4–6 cm.

Ratio

  • Dashi 5
  • Dark soy 3
  • Sugar 1
  • Mirin 1
  • + 3 cloves garlic
  • + 3 slices ginger

Enough liquid to submerge the fish. No sake.

Method

  1. Combine dashi, soy, sugar, mirin in a wide pot; add the garlic, whole, and the ginger.
  2. Add the fish pieces so they sit in one layer, fully covered.
  3. Bring up to a simmer, then keep it low and gentle — about 2 h. The liquid stays thin and brothy, not reduced to a glaze; the fish takes the colour deep into the flesh and the collar meat pulls off the bone.
  4. Serve a few pieces in a small bowl with a ladle of the liquid.

The 2 h is an estimate, not timed. Fillet pieces would fall apart long before that; the long simmer is for the bony collar and head pieces. Keeps in the liquid in the fridge; reheat gently.
