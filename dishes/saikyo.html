Salmon saikyo-yaki & yuan-yaki
西京焼き · 幽庵焼き

Sources: Board and notes 19 Oct. Draft.

Method

  1. Pieces 80–100 g.notes
  2. Saikyo: salt 10 min, rinse, 3 days in the miso cure.notes
  3. Yuan: 30 min in the yuzu-soy marinade.board
  4. Grill. No grilling notes were taken.curriculum

Both scorch easily because of sugar/mirin: wipe the marinade off, grill on low, skin side first, finish flesh side; or 200 °C oven ~10 min.
