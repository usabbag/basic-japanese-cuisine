Nizakana
煮魚 · simmered fish

  • Done in class with the split kinmedai head (kabuto-ni); works with any fish — heads, collars or fillets.

Ratio

  • Water 3
  • Sake 3
  • Dark soy 1
  • Mirin 1
  • Sugar 0.5

Method

  1. Head: blanch 5 s, iced water, remove the remaining scales under running water (see Kinmedai).
  2. Combine the liquid, add the fish, cook and boil down about 15 min.
