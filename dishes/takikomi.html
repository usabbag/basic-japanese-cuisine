Flavoured rice with kinmedai
炊き込みご飯 takikomi-gohan

  • Works with any fish, not just kinmedai: marinate the fillets, grill the skin, then cook on top of the rice.

Ingredients

  • Rice 450 g (540 ml)
  • Seasoning liquid, same volume as the rice (540 ml total):
    • Kombu stock (or water) 540 ml
    • Light soy 3 tbsp (45 ml)
    • Mirin 1 tbsp (15 ml)
    • Salt, a little
    • Ginger juice 1 tbsp (15 ml)
  • Kinmedai, 4 pieces (uwami fillet)
  • Aburaage 1 sheet, finely chopped

Method

  1. Marinate the fish in light soy and sake, 1 : 1.
  2. Grill the fish, skin side, before it goes in.
  3. Wash the rice (see Rice), add the seasoning liquid, the chopped aburaage and the grilled fish on top; cook as usual.
