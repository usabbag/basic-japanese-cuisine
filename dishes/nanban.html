Salmon nanban-zuke
南蛮漬け

Sources: Video 9-1; board 19 Oct. Draft.

Method

  1. Salmon skin on, bite-size pieces. Brush potato starch (katakuriko) on the flesh side.video 9-1
  2. Vegetables (paprika, three colours) fried plain (su-age) from ~160 °C — too hot and the skin lifts.video 9-1
  3. Salmon fried hotter, 170–180 °C.video 9-1
  4. Marinade to a full boil; pour boiling over the fried pieces until all is submerged. Paper over the top. Cool to room temperature.video 9-1
  5. Plate (3–4 servings per plate); ladle marinade over; shiraga-negi in the centre, a touch of ichimi.video 9-1

Keeps 2–3 days in the fridge and is better the next day; serve cold or at room temperature as an appetizer or bento item.
